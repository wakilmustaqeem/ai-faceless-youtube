# Episode 01 — Shot 02 Presenter Entry Local Production Lock v1

## Purpose
Continue directly from Shot 01 without changing classroom geography or the three-display geometry.

## Shot
- Duration target: 4–6 seconds.
- Format: native 9:16.
- Era: 2040.
- Same classroom, same students, same displays and lighting as Shot 01.
- Full-body realistic human presenter enters naturally from the classroom side aisle.
- Students notice him with subtle eye/head movement; no exaggerated reaction.
- Presenter walks to the center teaching position, stops with believable weight shift and looks toward the center display.
- Center display remains physically wall-mounted; left/right displays remain fixed.

## Performance
Natural walking cadence, arm swing, breathing, blinking, small posture corrections and eye-line changes. The presenter must feel like a real teacher entering a real classroom, not an AI avatar.

## Dialogue timing
After the presenter settles into position: “Today… we don't play the game. We build it.”
Keep delivery calm, confident and cinematic. Do not reveal the time-travel story in this shot.

## Prompt
Photorealistic live-action cinematic 2040 international technology classroom, same established classroom geometry, three fixed wall-mounted digital displays, same diverse student group seated naturally, a realistic full-body male technology teacher enters through the side aisle and walks naturally toward the center teaching position, authentic human gait, subtle breathing and blinking, natural eye contact with students and center display, believable weight shift, premium modern classroom, physically grounded furniture and screens, realistic shadows and reflections, restrained cinematic camera tracking movement, natural classroom lighting, professional international education film, continuous visual identity with the previous shot.

## Negative prompt
AI avatar, plastic skin, mannequin movement, floating screens, changing room layout, extra limbs, duplicate students, face drift, identity drift, teleporting, exaggerated gestures, cartoon, anime, CGI human, slideshow motion, impossible walking, horror, war, weapons.

## Continuity gate
Reject if presenter identity changes, body proportions drift, classroom geometry changes, displays move/fly, students suddenly change seats/clothes, or the walk looks synthetic.

Human approval required before assembly.
