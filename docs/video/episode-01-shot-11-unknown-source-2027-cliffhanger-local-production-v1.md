# Episode 01 — Shot 11: UNKNOWN SOURCE / 2027 — Cliffhanger Lock v1

## Purpose
End Episode 1 with the first concrete clue that the LEGACY CORE signal is connected to another time period, without explaining the time bridge.

## Duration
Target 6–10 seconds.

## Action
1. Center LCD remains on the restrained **LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED** diagnostic.
2. A new source-resolution line appears:
   **UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027**
3. The source line stabilizes briefly.
4. Presenter looks at the master mobile, then back at the center display.
5. The students remain silent and attentive; one or two exchange a natural glance.
6. Presenter does not touch the display yet.
7. The master mobile shows the same unknown-source alert.
8. Hold for a short beat.
9. Abrupt but clean cut to black.
10. End card/audio cue prepares the next episode: **PART 2**.

## Dialogue
No required dialogue.

Optional presenter whisper:
“2027?”

Do not explain who or what is in 2027.

## Visual continuity
- Same 2040 classroom from Shots 1–10.
- Same presenter, Student 07 and student group.
- Exactly three physically fixed displays.
- Same master mobile.
- No visible time portal.
- No time-machine device.
- No historical character reveal.
- No family reveal.
- No 2090 signal.
- No causal-loop explanation.
- The only new story fact is the year **2027**.

## Visual prompt
“Photorealistic cinematic live-action 2040 international technology classroom, exact continuity from the previous successful racing sequence, same realistic human presenter, same Student 07 and student group, exactly three physically fixed wall-mounted LCD displays, center display showing a restrained futuristic diagnostic interface reading ‘LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED’, a new clean source-resolution line appears: ‘UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027’, presenter naturally shifts gaze from the center display to his master mobile and back, students become quietly attentive and exchange subtle believable glances, realistic human breathing blinking and eye movement, premium restrained future classroom lighting, cinematic slow push-in, controlled suspense, grounded science-fiction realism, no visible time machine, no portal.”

## Audio
- Classroom room tone continues.
- One restrained diagnostic tone when “UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027” resolves.
- Brief near-silence immediately before cut to black.
- No horror sting.
- No loud alarm.
- After cut to black, a short clean transition cue may introduce “PART 2”.

## Editing / cliffhanger
The cut to black must happen quickly after the audience has had enough time to read **2027**.

Do not add explanatory narration.

The audience should leave Episode 1 with one clear question:
**Why is a 2040 classroom receiving a signal from 2027?**

## Acceptance gate
PASS only if:
- “UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027” is clearly readable.
- 2027 is the only new time-related reveal.
- Presenter and students react naturally, not theatrically.
- Exactly three fixed displays remain.
- No portal/time-machine imagery appears.
- Continuity with Shots 1–10 is preserved.
- The cut to black feels intentional and immediate.
- Episode 1 does not explain the mystery.
- Human owner approves before final assembly.
