# Episode 01 — Shot 10: LEGACY CORE / Strange Signal — Local Production Lock v1

## Purpose
After the successful first lap, introduce the first unexplained technology signal without revealing the full time-travel truth.

## Duration
Target 7–12 seconds.

## Story position
This is the transition from the successful race payoff into the Episode 1 mystery/cliffhanger.

## Action
1. The successful race remains visible for a brief moment.
2. Center display subtly flickers once.
3. The race UI pauses without a crash.
4. Left LCD telemetry fades to a diagnostic state.
5. Right LCD track view dims.
6. A restrained system diagnostic appears on the center display:
   **LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED**
7. A small status line indicates that the signal source is outside the current classroom system.
8. Presenter notices the anomaly and looks from the center display to the master mobile.
9. Students become quietly attentive; no panic.
10. The classroom remains physically unchanged.
11. End on the mysterious diagnostic state, ready for Shot 11.

## Dialogue
No required dialogue.
Optional presenter reaction, very quiet:
“That's new.”

Do not explain what LEGACY CORE means in Episode 1.

## Visual continuity
- Same 2040 classroom.
- Same presenter identity and wardrobe.
- Same Student 07 and student group.
- Exactly three physically fixed LCDs: center, left, right.
- Same racing game UI and car.
- Same master mobile.
- No floating displays.
- No holographic time portal yet.
- No visible time-machine imagery.
- No 2027 reveal until Shot 11.
