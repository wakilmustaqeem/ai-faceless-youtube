# Episode 02 — Shot Prompts v1

All shots: native 9:16, cinematic realistic live-action look, same locked cast and locations, natural human motion, no avatar/cartoon/plastic skin, no floating displays.

## Shot 01 — The Signal Returns
Duration: 4–6 sec
2040 classroom, same geography as Episode 1. Center LCD wakes from black:
LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED
UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027
Presenter looks at the display, then quietly says: “Why 2027?”
Students exchange restrained curious looks.
Camera: slow push-in, medium/full-body presenter coverage.
Audio: room tone, restrained diagnostic pulse.

## Shot 02 — Application Takes Control
Duration: 5–7 sec
Presenter raises the master mobile. The Time-Travel Application opens.
Mobile UI:
SOURCE YEAR: 2027
DESTINATION AVAILABLE
Three fixed LCDs react as a connected system.
Students naturally lean forward.
Camera: over-shoulder mobile → center display → presenter.
No holographic screens.

## Shot 03 — Year Selection
Duration: 5–7 sec
Presenter selects 2027.
Center LCD:
ERA SCAN — 2027
DESTINATION: READY
Left LCD shows timeline/data scan; right LCD shows a simplified destination map.
Presenter's hand gesture confirms the selection.
Camera: controlled medium shot with readable UI.
No teleport yet.

## Shot 04 — First Teleport: Departure
Duration: 4–6 sec
Presenter confirms destination.
The permanent teleport signature begins:
YEAR SELECT → DESTINATION CONFIRM → bright white/golden light burst.
Controlled particles, subtle distortion and motion blur.
At peak brightness the presenter disappears naturally.
Students remain in place and react with believable surprise.
No portal, no horror, no magic aesthetic.

## Shot 05 — 2027 Arrival
Duration: 5–7 sec
2027 environment, visually distinct from the 2040 classroom.
Matching white/golden arrival burst.
Light contracts toward the presenter as he materializes with correct footing.
Screen/UI resolves:
NEW WORLD
YEAR: 2027
ERA: PAST
Presenter breathes naturally and looks around.
Destination ambience starts immediately.

## Shot 06 — First Contact
Duration: 7–9 sec
Presenter notices a young Pakistani creator working at a modest 2027 desk setup.
Creator notices the presenter.
Both pause.
Natural eye contact, cautious curiosity, then a friendly expression.
No threatening body language.
Camera: alternating close-ups with a brief full-body establishing view.

## Shot 07 — The Racing Game Connection
Duration: 7–9 sec
Creator's monitor shows an early racing-game prototype.
Presenter recognizes the architecture.
Close-up: steering logic.
Close-up: creator's hand and keyboard.
A matching signal appears on both systems.
Presenter quietly: “This is the same system.”
Creator looks surprised.
Keep UI readable but avoid excessive tiny text.

## Shot 08 — Friendly Talent Exchange
Duration: 8–10 sec
Presenter demonstrates a modern debugging method.
Creator immediately shows an original racing-game design idea from 2027.
They work side by side.
Their screens exchange a small authorized data packet.
Theme is visible through action: future technique meets past creativity.
Warm, collaborative performance.

## Shot 09 — Time Bridge Instability
Duration: 6–8 sec
The Time-Travel Application suddenly displays:
TIME BRIDGE INSTABILITY
RETURN CONTROL: TEMPORARILY UNAVAILABLE
Presenter and creator look at the warning.
No alarm panic.
Camera slowly pushes toward the warning while both remain in frame.
Audio: low electronic tension, not horror.
