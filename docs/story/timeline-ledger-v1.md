## Object Ledger Rule
Every time-travelled object must have:
1. Origin year
2. Destination year
3. Authorized sender
4. Authorized receiver
5. Transfer purpose
6. Quantity limit
7. Return/disposition status
8. Whether its transfer creates a causal loop

No object may appear in another era without a ledger entry.
