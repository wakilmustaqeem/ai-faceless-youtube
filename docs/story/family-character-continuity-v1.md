# FAMILY CHARACTER CONTINUITY BIBLE v1

## Permanent Family Unit

| Character | Relationship | 2027 Age | Primary Story Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Wakil | Father of Mustafa and Mujtaba | Story-flexible | Original mastermind of the Time-Travel App; father, guide and protector |
| Mustafa | Son of Wakil; elder brother of Mujtaba | 18 | Main HERO; recipient of the Time-Travel App gift; primary adventurer |
| Mujtaba | Son of Wakil; younger brother of Mustafa | 13 | Younger brother; curious, intelligent recurring character |

## Core Story Triangle

**Wakil builds the door.**  
**Wakil gifts the Time-Travel App to Mustafa.**  
**Mustafa becomes the hero.**  
**Mujtaba grows alongside his brother and becomes part of the family adventure.**

## Mustafa — Hero Lock

Mustafa must remain the main protagonist.

His story responsibilities:
- active time travel;
- mission decisions;
- glitch response;
- helping stranded travelers;
- cross-era friendships;
- learning from different generations;
- facing consequences;
- discovering the deeper truth about the application and his father.

## Wakil — Mastermind Lock

Wakil is the original mastermind behind the Time-Travel Mobile Application.

He may:
- explain hidden system knowledge;
- provide strategic guidance;
- protect his sons;
- intervene when a serious situation requires him;
- reveal pieces of the application's deeper architecture.

He must not replace Mustafa as the protagonist.

## Mujtaba — Younger Brother Lock

Mujtaba is 13 in 2027.

His characterization:
- curious;
- intelligent;
- energetic;
- observant;
- emotionally connected to Mustafa;
- willing to ask the question nobody else noticed.

His contribution can include:
- spotting clues;
- asking useful questions;
- understanding simple interfaces;
- helping with safe/authorized device tasks;
- learning from Mustafa;
- adding family warmth and emotional stakes.

Mujtaba does not receive unrestricted access to protected Time-Travel functions merely because he is family. Age-appropriate safety and story authorization remain in force.

## Brother Relationship

Mustafa and Mujtaba should feel like real brothers.

Continuity:
- Mustafa is the elder brother and often guides/protects Mujtaba.
- Mujtaba respects Mustafa but is not required to agree with him.
- Mujtaba can challenge Mustafa with smart observations.
- Mustafa can learn from Mujtaba's fresh perspective.
- Their relationship should create warmth, humor and emotional stakes without weakening the cinematic adventure.

## Time-Travel Continuity

When the family appears in different years, each character's:
- wardrobe;
- hair/styling;
- props;
- technology;
- transportation;
- environment;
- language/register;
- money/payment context;
- ambient sound

must match the destination era.

The application may prepare the traveler for the destination year, but character identity and family relationships remain continuous.

## Hero / Mastermind / Brother Rule

Never write:
- Wakil as Mustafa's replacement hero;
- Mujtaba as Mustafa's replacement hero;
- Mujtaba as older than Mustafa;
- Mustafa as a side character;
- the Time-Travel App as unrelated to Wakil's gift.

Always preserve:

**Wakil = father + mastermind + giver**  
**Mustafa = 18 + elder brother + hero**  
**Mujtaba = 13 + younger brother + recurring family character**

## Production Note

For realistic cinematic generation, the three family members must retain stable identity references across shots and episodes. Do not allow age drift, relationship drift, or accidental role swapping.
