# MASTER MIND & TIME-TRAVEL APP GIFT CANON v1

## Core Story Truth

**Wakil is the original mastermind behind the Time-Travel Mobile Application.**

Wakil develops/conceives the time-travel application and later **gifts it to his son Mustafa**.

**Mustafa remains the HERO and central protagonist of the universe.**

The gift does not replace Mustafa's role. It is the reason Mustafa receives the extraordinary opportunity to become the hero who learns, travels, protects people, solves glitches and builds relationships across eras.

## Father → Son Dynamic

**Wakil → creates/masterminds the Time-Travel Application → gifts it to Mustafa → Mustafa becomes the hero and active time-traveler.**

Wakil is the hidden/strategic mastermind and father figure.

Mustafa is the visible adventure hero.

This distinction must remain consistent across episodes.

## Character Authority

### Wakil
- Mustafa's father.
- Original mastermind/designer of the Time-Travel Mobile Application.
- Creates the core concept and safeguards the deeper architecture.
- Gifts the application to Mustafa.
- Protective, wise, kind and technically capable.
- Can understand the deeper system and guide Mustafa when necessary.
- His role is strategic and foundational, not a replacement for the hero.

### Mustafa
- Wakil's son.
- Main hero/protagonist.
- 18 years old and Matric student in the 2027 origin timeline.
- Receives the Time-Travel Mobile Application as a gift from Wakil.
- Becomes the primary active traveler and problem-solver.
- Travels to past, present and future by selecting a year in the application.
- Protects people affected by glitches.
- Builds friendships across eras.
- Learns from every era while helping others.
- Remains the emotional and adventure center of the story.

## Time-Travel Application

The application is a major father-to-son gift.

Basic flow:

**Open App → Select Year → Confirm → Era Scan → Time Gate → Destination**

The destination year determines:
- wardrobe
- environment
- architecture
- technology
- devices and props
- transportation
- money/payment context
- food/everyday objects
- language/register where relevant
- ambient sound
- social/visual context

The app can access **PAST / PRESENT / FUTURE**, subject to story authorization and protected Time Gates.

## Glitch Rule

When a time-travel glitch affects Mustafa or other travelers:

**GLITCH → MUSTAFA ARRIVES/ACTS → MUSTAFA HELPS → PEOPLE BECOME FRIENDS → NEW MISSION**

Wakil may guide, warn or intervene strategically, but Mustafa remains the on-screen hero who faces the adventure.

## Mystery Structure

The audience should not receive the full Wakil-mastermind truth immediately.

Early episodes may present the app as a mysterious gift.

Later episodes reveal:
1. Wakil built the original concept.
2. Wakil deliberately chose Mustafa as the recipient.
3. The gift contains deeper safeguards and hidden architecture.
4. Mustafa discovers that his father understood the Time Bridge long before he did.
5. The father-son relationship becomes part of the larger mystery.

## Continuity Lock

Never write:
- Wakil replaces Mustafa as hero.
- Wakil is the original hero instead of Mustafa.
- Mustafa is merely a side character.
- Mustafa loses the protagonist role because Wakil created the app.

Always write:
**Wakil = mastermind/father/giver**
**Mustafa = son/hero/user/adventurer**

## Theme

**"A father built the door. A son became the hero who walked through it."**

This is the canonical father-son story engine for the Racing Game / Time-Travel Universe.
