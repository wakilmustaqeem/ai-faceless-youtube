# FAMILY CANON — WAKIL, MUSTAFA & MUJTABA v2

## Family Relationship Lock

**Wakil = father**  
**Mustafa = son / hero**  
**Mujtaba = son / Mustafa's younger brother**

The three are a permanent family unit in the Racing Game / Time-Travel Universe.

## 2027 Family Ages

- **Mustafa — 18 years old**, Matric student and main hero.
- **Mujtaba — 13 years old**, Mustafa's younger brother.
- **Wakil — father**; exact age remains story-flexible unless separately locked.

## Wakil

Wakil is the original mastermind behind the Time-Travel Mobile Application.

He develops the deeper Time-Travel concept/system and gifts the application to Mustafa.

Wakil remains:
- father;
- mastermind;
- strategic guide;
- protective family figure;
- keeper of deeper system knowledge.

Wakil does **not** replace Mustafa as the hero.

## Mustafa

Mustafa is:
- Wakil's son;
- Mujtaba's elder brother;
- 18 years old in 2027;
- Matric student;
- the main HERO/protagonist;
- recipient of Wakil's Time-Travel Application gift;
- primary active time-traveler and problem-solver.

Mustafa remains the emotional and adventure center of the story.

## Mujtaba

Mujtaba is:
- Wakil's son;
- Mustafa's younger brother;
- **13 years old in 2027**;
- intelligent, curious and energetic;
- a recurring family character;
- closely connected to Mustafa's adventures.

Mujtaba is not a replacement for Mustafa's hero role.

His character can naturally contribute through:
- curiosity and questions;
- noticing details others miss;
- helping with devices and interfaces;
- learning from Mustafa;
- providing younger-family perspective;
- participating in safe, authorized missions when the story calls for it.

His involvement must respect the age of the character and the story's safety/authorization rules.

## Family Story Dynamic

**Wakil builds the door → Wakil gifts the application to Mustafa → Mustafa becomes the hero → Mujtaba becomes the younger brother who grows up alongside the adventure.**

The brothers should have a genuine sibling relationship:
- Mustafa protects and guides Mujtaba.
- Mujtaba looks up to Mustafa but can challenge him with smart questions.
- They learn from each other.
- Their relationship can create emotional moments during time-travel missions.

## Permanent Continuity Rules

Never change:
- Mustafa's role as the main hero.
- Mujtaba's role as Mustafa's younger brother.
- Mujtaba's 2027 age of 13.
- Wakil's role as their father and the original mastermind behind the Time-Travel Application.

Any future episode involving the family must preserve these relationships unless a clearly marked story event intentionally changes them.

## Theme

**Father, hero son, younger brother — one family connected across time.**
