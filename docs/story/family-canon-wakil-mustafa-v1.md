# FAMILY CANON — WAKIL & MUSTAFA v1

## Relationship Lock
Wakil is Mustafa's father.

## Creator Timeline
- 2027: Mustafa is 18 years old, a Matric student and the original creator of the Racing Game / Time-Travel APK.
- Wakil: Mustafa's father and the trusted family figure who helps protect Mustafa and people affected by temporal glitches.
- Mustafa retains the original creator identity and the true Secret Core / Root Key.
- Wakil is not a replacement for Mustafa's creator role.

## Character Roles

### Mustafa
- Original creator.
- 18 years old in 2027.
- Matric student.
- Holds the deepest creator knowledge of the original APK.
- Root/Secret Core authority remains his unless an explicit story event changes it.

### Wakil
- Mustafa's father.
- Kind, sensible, responsible and protective.
- Helps people during Time-Travel glitches.
- Becomes a trusted friend and mentor figure across eras.
- Can participate in missions and rescue travelers.
- Does not casually override Mustafa's creator authority.

## Story Dynamic
The father-son relationship is central to the universe:
**Mustafa creates the system → Wakil protects people → glitches bring them into wider time-travel adventures.**

People rescued by Wakil may become friends with both Wakil and Mustafa, creating cross-generation relationships and future missions.

## Canon Correction
Previous canon that described Wakil as replacing Mustafa as the original creator is superseded by this document.

The canonical family relationship is:
**Wakil = father**
**Mustafa = son / original creator**
