# Racing Game — Access Ledger v2

## FAMILY — FULL ACCESS + FULL CONTROL

The complete family has full authorized control of the Time-Travel system:

- Wakil — FULL
- Walida — FULL
- Mustafa — FULL
- Mujtaba — FULL
- Khadija — FULL
- Qirat — FULL

Every family member may:
- select any destination year;
- travel to past, present or future;
- return to a selected year;
- operate Time Gates;
- inspect era/time data;
- use the Time-Travel Mobile Application;
- control authorized time-travel functions;
- respond to glitches;
- assist people across eras;
- access Time-Travel missions and Time Commerce;
- use personal control devices, including Mujtaba's smartwatch.

No family member has a permanent LIMITED or BLOCKED state.

## FAMILY CONTROL PRINCIPLE

**Family access is shared full authority.**

Age changes characterization, responsibility and scene behavior, but does not remove authorized system control from any family member.

## HERO PRINCIPLE

Full control is shared by the family, but protagonist status is not.

**Mustafa remains the MAIN HERO.**

Mujtaba, Khadija and Qirat may independently trigger major story events because they possess full access. Wakil and Walida retain complete control and can intervene directly.

## MUJTABA SPECIAL ACCESS

Mujtaba additionally has:
- updated Time-Travel code in his smartwatch;
- a private secret code;
- full system access through the smartwatch.

His secret code is an additional protected mechanism, not a restriction on other family members.

## 2040 FUTURE TEAM

The 2040 students/team remain session-limited unless a later canon explicitly changes their status.

They can use:
- approved Racing Engine modules;
- approved updates;
- approved Time Gates;
- session-limited student devices.

They cannot automatically inherit the family's full control.

## 2090 EVOLVED SYSTEM

2090 users/modules remain authorization-governed.

Cross-era messaging and evolved modules are allowed where the story authorizes them.

The family's full control is preserved.

## TIME COMMERCE

Family members may initiate or participate in authorized Future→Past and Past→Future exchanges.

The family can decide what to send, receive, exchange, or investigate.

## GLITCH / EMERGENCY

A glitch does not remove family authority.

Any family member may:
1. detect the glitch;
2. enter the affected era;
3. operate the Time Gate;
4. help affected people;
5. stabilize or redirect the mission;
6. return when ready.

Emergency systems should protect people and continuity without permanently locking the family out.

## Continuity Lock

Never:
- downgrade a family member to LIMITED without an explicit new canon;
- remove full family Time-Travel control;
- make Mujtaba's secret code the only family access mechanism;
- confuse full system control with protagonist status;
- remove Mustafa's MAIN HERO role.

## Canon Status

This v2 ledger supersedes the earlier access ledger wherever it conflicts with the latest family full-access/full-control canon.
