# RACING GAME — EPISODE 01 ASSET BIBLE v1

## Production Principle
Continuity is treated as production data, not a prompt wish. Every recurring human, prop and location gets a locked reference before shot generation. Reference-driven workflows are recommended for maintaining identity across independent clips.

## Asset IDs

### CHAR-PRESENTER-2040
Real human male presenter, adult, professional future educator.
- Same face, hair, body proportions and wardrobe in every Episode 1 shot.
- Calm confidence; natural blinking/breathing.
- Full-body reference + clean face reference required.
- Wardrobe: premium dark neutral smart-casual teaching outfit; no logos.
- Prop ownership: master mobile always belongs to presenter.

### CHAR-STUDENT-GROUP-2040
Recurring mixed student group.
- Same faces, hairstyles, body proportions and clothing palette across shots.
- Natural age-appropriate classroom behavior.
- No synchronized/exaggerated reactions.
- Student devices remain visually consistent.

### PROP-MASTER-MOBILE
Presenter's private control console.
- Thin premium handheld device.
- Same physical dimensions, finish and interface language.
- Never changes hands during Episode 1.

### LOCATION-CLASSROOM-2040
Premium international classroom.
- Center: one large fixed wall display.
- Left: one fixed LCD angled inward.
- Right: one fixed LCD angled inward.
- Student desks face center display.
- Displays are physically installed; never floating.
- Consistent room geometry, lighting direction and seating.

### UI-RACING-CORE
Visual language:
- premium technical UI
- clean dark interface with restrained luminous accents
- readable diagrams
- 3D racing track
- steering/input visualization
- no cheap neon cyberpunk
- no fantasy hologram panels

### UI-MYSTERY-LEGACY
Only appears in final seconds:
- LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED
- UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027
- Do not expose Secret Core or Root Key.

## Shot Prompt System
Use the locked references and change primarily the action/camera instruction. Do not reinvent identity in every shot. Keep camera language, lighting direction and classroom geometry stable. This follows current continuity guidance that identity, camera and world/light should be locked while shot action changes.

## Universal Negative
No face morphing, no identity drift, no wardrobe changes, no extra students, no duplicated limbs, no floating screens, no impossible hand interaction, no plastic skin, no cartoon, no avatar look, no slideshow, no watermark, no random logos, no unexplained props.

## QA Gate
A clip fails and must be regenerated if:
- presenter identity changes;
- student identities change materially;
- classroom screen geometry changes;
- master mobile changes;
- lighting direction jumps without story reason;
- a fixed LCD becomes floating;
- UI responds without a physical/voice/gesture cause;
- mystery text appears before the final beat;
- continuity between adjacent clips is broken.

## Reference-to-Video Rule
Generate short clips shot-by-shot from approved references, then inspect transition frames before accepting the next clip. Current 2026 guidance also emphasizes locked references, shot-level continuity and review/repair rather than relying on text prompts alone.
