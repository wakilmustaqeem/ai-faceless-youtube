# Racing Game — Episode Map v1

## Format
Target: vertical 9:16 cinematic micro-drama. Episodes default to 60–120 seconds, with a fast hook and unresolved end. Current industry guidance commonly uses this range and hook/cliffhanger structure. 

## Arc 1 — THE FIRST LAP
1. **The First Lap** — 2040 classroom; students update Racing Game; first live race; steering bug.
2. **The First Contact** — Time Bridge activates; 2027 past creator-side connection appears.
3. **The Year That Should Not Exist** — 2090 signal appears.
4. **The Message from 2090** — 2090 generation says the game becomes important.
5. **The Original Creator** — system identifies Mustafa, 18, Matric, 2027.
6. **Root Access** — Future team discovers they have only Limited Access.
7. **The Secret Core** — encrypted Time-Travel Core is detected but remains locked.
8. **The Time Market** — controlled Future→Past transfer is opened.
9. **The 2090 Device** — a device sent to 2027 carries a 2090 origin signature.
10. **Creator-Only Market** — Root-only market appears; Mustafa must decide what to unlock.

## Arc 2 — TWO-WAY EXCHANGE
11. Future technology reaches 2027.
12. Mustafa sells/exchanges an authorized future device.
13. Past talent/design reaches 2040.
14. Future students discover unexpected value in a past-origin product.
15. A transfer limit is reached.
16. A new Time Gate opens.
17. Past and future teams solve one problem together.
18. 2090 requests a specific original artifact.
19. Mustafa refuses full access.
20. The APK creates a new branch.

## Arc 3 — THE CREATOR'S SECRET
21. Mustafa's hidden development notebook is discovered.
22. The first Secret Core prototype is revealed.
23. The Future team learns why the Core was restricted.
24. A controlled causal loop is detected.
25. Mustafa receives information apparently sent by his future self.
26. The team must verify the message without rewriting history.
27. Root Access challenge.
28. Mustafa unlocks one temporary module.
29. The Time Bridge reveals a fourth destination.
30. **UNKNOWN ERA** — season-level cliffhanger.

## Continuity Ledger Rules
- Mustafa is 18 and a Matric student in 2027.
- Mustafa remains the original creator and Root authority.
- Future access stays limited unless a story event explicitly changes it.
- Secret Core is never casually exposed.
- Time Bridge is friendly and controlled.
- Time commerce is two-way and quantity-limited.
- History is not casually rewritten.
- 2040 classroom technology stays physically consistent.
- Every episode must add a new story fact, mission, consequence or mystery.
