# FAMILY + TIME-TRAVEL CANON v2

## Family

| Character | Relationship | Age in 2027 | Canon Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Wakil | Father | Story-flexible | Original mastermind behind the Time-Travel Mobile Application; gifted it to Mustafa |
| Mustafa | Wakil's son; Mujtaba's elder brother | 18 | Main HERO and primary adventurer |
| Mujtaba | Wakil's son; Mustafa's younger brother | 13 | Full-access time traveler, smartwatch developer, holder of a secret code |
| Walida | Mother of Mustafa and Mujtaba | Story-flexible | Family anchor, wise/supportive parent and recurring character |
| Younger Sister 1 | Daughter of Wakil and Walida; younger sister of Mustafa/Mujtaba | Story-flexible | Recurring younger-sister character |

Names and exact ages of Walida and the two younger sisters remain open until explicitly named.

## Mujtaba Full-Access Upgrade

Mujtaba is no longer limited-access.

He successfully updates the Time-Travel code inside his smartwatch and gains **full authorized access** to the Time-Travel system.

His smartwatch becomes a legitimate personal control device:
- select destination year;
- initiate authorized time travel;
- return to a selected year;
- inspect era/time data;
- monitor Time Gate status;
- use approved Time-Travel functions;
- receive system alerts;
- operate his own authorized travel sessions.

### Secret Code

Mujtaba possesses a separate **secret code** known only to him and the protected system.

The code is not a replacement for the application's architecture or Wakil's original authorship. It is a hidden authorization/key layer created or discovered through Mujtaba's smartwatch update.

Story rule:
- Mujtaba does not casually reveal the secret code.
- The secret code can unlock a mystery, emergency route, protected function or hidden Time Gate when the story requires it.
- The code must not automatically override every safety/continuity rule.
- Its existence should be revealed gradually.

## Hero Hierarchy

**Wakil = original mastermind + father + giver**  
**Mustafa = MAIN HERO + elder brother**  
**Mujtaba = full-access younger brother + smartwatch coder + secret-code holder**

Full access does NOT make Mujtaba the main hero.

Mustafa remains the central protagonist. Mujtaba becomes a powerful co-adventurer whose actions can trigger major plot turns.

## Glitch Rule

When a time-travel glitch occurs:

**GLITCH → MUSTAFA ACTS → MUJTABA SUPPORTS/UNLOCKS → PEOPLE ARE HELPED → FRIENDSHIP → NEW MISSION**

Either brother may be physically present in a mission. Their roles should complement rather than replace one another.

## Walida

Walida is a real recurring family character.

She provides:
- emotional grounding;
- family wisdom;
- practical judgment;
- concern for the boys' safety;
- support during major discoveries;
- continuity between adventure and home life.

She can know important family history, but her knowledge should be revealed naturally rather than as exposition.

## Two Younger Sisters

The two younger sisters are recurring family members.

They can:
- observe the brothers' missions;
- ask innocent but insightful questions;
- notice family clues adults overlook;
- participate in safe, age-appropriate moments;
- add warmth, humor and emotional stakes.

They do not automatically receive unrestricted Time-Travel access. Their access is determined by story authorization and age-appropriate safety unless a later canon explicitly changes it.

## Era Adaptation

Every traveler and family member must visually and contextually adapt to the destination year:
- wardrobe;
- hair/styling;
- devices;
- props;
- architecture;
- transportation;
- money/payment;
- language/register;
- food/everyday objects;
- ambient sound;
- screen/UI styling.

Identity and family relationships remain continuous across eras.

## Core Theme

**A father built the door. He gifted it to his son. His sons discovered new ways through it. The family became part of the adventure.**

## Continuity Lock

Never:
- remove Mustafa's hero status;
- make Mujtaba's full access equal to protagonist status;
- forget Mujtaba's smartwatch update;
- forget Mujtaba's secret code;
- make the sisters older than the brothers;
- make Walida unrelated to the family;
- treat Wakil as merely a side character.

Always preserve the family structure and role hierarchy above.

## Canon Status

This file supersedes older family wording where it conflicts with the latest explicit user-approved canon, while preserving the broader Racing Game time-travel universe.
