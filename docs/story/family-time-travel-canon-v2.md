# FAMILY + TIME-TRAVEL CANON v2

|---|---|---:|---|

## Age Lock

In 2027:
- Wakil = 35
- Walida = 35
- Mustafa = 18
- Mujtaba = 13
- Khadija = 14
- Qirat = 10

Khadija and Qirat are younger sisters in the family continuity. Their ages are now explicitly locked.

## Family Full-Access + Full-Control Lock

All six family members have **full authorized Time-Travel access and control**:
- Wakil
- Walida
- Mustafa
- Mujtaba
- Khadija
- Qirat

Every family member can, according to the shared system controls:
- select any destination year;
- travel to past, present or future;
- return to any selected year;
- operate Time Gates;
- inspect era/time data;
- control authorized time-travel functions;
- receive system alerts;
- use the Time-Travel Mobile Application;
- use their own authorized control device;
- respond to glitches and help others.

There is no family member with permanently restricted access. The family trusts one another and shares the system's full control authority.

## Mujtaba Full-Access Upgrade

Mujtaba is no longer limited-access.

At age 13, he successfully updates the Time-Travel code inside his smartwatch and gains **full authorized access** to the Time-Travel system.

His smartwatch becomes a legitimate personal control device:
- select destination year;
- initiate authorized time travel;
- return to a selected year;
- inspect era/time data;
- monitor Time Gate status;
- use approved Time-Travel functions;
- receive system alerts;
- operate his own authorized travel sessions.

### Secret Code

Mujtaba possesses a separate **secret code** known only to him and the protected system.

The code is not a replacement for the application's architecture or Wakil's original authorship. It is a hidden authorization/key layer created or discovered through Mujtaba's smartwatch update.

Story rule:
- Mujtaba does not casually reveal the secret code.
- The secret code can unlock a mystery, emergency route, protected function or hidden Time Gate when the story requires it.
- The code must not automatically override every safety/continuity rule.
- Its existence should be revealed gradually.

## Hero Hierarchy

**Wakil = 35 + original mastermind + father + giver**  
**Walida = 35 + mother + family anchor**  
**Mustafa = 18 + MAIN HERO + elder brother**  
**Mujtaba = 13 + full-access younger brother + smartwatch coder + secret-code holder**  
**Khadija = 14 + younger sister**  
**Qirat = 10 + younger sister**

Full access does NOT change the hero hierarchy.

Mustafa remains the central protagonist. Mujtaba, Khadija and Qirat can all independently initiate major story actions because they have full family control authority. Wakil and Walida also retain complete control and can intervene directly whenever they choose.

## Glitch Rule

When a time-travel glitch occurs:

**GLITCH → MUSTAFA ACTS → MUJTABA SUPPORTS/UNLOCKS → PEOPLE ARE HELPED → FRIENDSHIP → NEW MISSION**

Either brother may be physically present in a mission. Their roles should complement rather than replace one another.

## Walida

Walida is a real recurring family character.

She provides:
- emotional grounding;
- family wisdom;
- practical judgment;
- concern for the boys' safety;
- support during major discoveries;
- continuity between adventure and home life.

She can know important family history, but her knowledge should be revealed naturally rather than as exposition.

## Younger Sisters

Khadija and Qirat are recurring family members.

They can:
- observe the brothers' missions;
- ask innocent but insightful questions;
- notice family clues adults overlook;
- participate in safe, age-appropriate moments;
- add warmth, humor and emotional stakes.

Khadija and Qirat have the same full family access and control as the rest of the family. Their younger age affects how they are portrayed in scenes, judgment and responsibility, but it does not remove their authorized Time-Travel control.

## Era Adaptation

Every traveler and family member must visually and contextually adapt to the destination year:
- wardrobe;
- hair/styling;
- devices;
- props;
- architecture;
- transportation;
- money/payment;
- language/register;
- food/everyday objects;
- ambient sound;
- screen/UI styling.

Identity and family relationships remain continuous across eras.

## Core Theme

**A father built the door. He gifted it to his son. His sons discovered new ways through it. The family became part of the adventure.**

## Continuity Lock

Never:
- remove Mustafa's hero status;
- remove full-access/full-control authority from any family member;
- forget Mujtaba's smartwatch update;
- forget Mujtaba's secret code;
- make Khadija or Qirat older than Mustafa;
- make Walida unrelated to the family;
- treat Wakil as merely a side character.

Always preserve the family structure and role hierarchy above.
