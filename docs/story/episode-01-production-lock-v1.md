# RACING GAME — EPISODE 01 PRODUCTION LOCK v1

## Identity
- Format: cinematic vertical micro-drama, native 9:16
- Era: 2040
- Classroom: premium international future classroom
- Core geometry: one large center wall display + fixed left LCD angled inward + fixed right LCD angled inward
- Control: presenter master mobile + voice + mid-air hand gestures
- Presenter: real human, full body, natural movement/voice
- Students: real humans, natural classroom behavior
- Visual rule: believable installed technology; no floating screens, cartoon, plastic skin, avatar look or cheap cyberpunk

## Canon
- Racing Game is secretly the interface of a time-travel APK.
- The original creator is Mustafa, age 18 in 2027, a Matric student.
- Mustafa retains Root authority and the Secret Core.
- 2040 users have Limited Access.
- Episode 1 must NOT reveal the full time-travel truth.

## Shot Plan

### 0–3 sec — HOOK
Black screen. A low electronic pulse.
Center display suddenly wakes:
**CAN YOU BUILD A RACING GAME?**
Left LCD: live input diagram.
Right LCD: empty track.

### 3–12 sec — THE CLASS
Wide cinematic classroom shot.
Students turn toward the displays. Presenter walks naturally to center position, holding the master mobile.
Presenter:
**“Today… we don't play the game. We build it.”**

### 12–28 sec — BUILD
Presenter gives a voice command:
**“Start build mode.”**
Three displays synchronize.
Center: game architecture.
Left: steering/input flow.
Right: 3D race track.
He uses a controlled hand gesture; the UI responds.

### 28–42 sec — FIRST TEST
Presenter:
**“Run the first lap.”**
A virtual car launches.
Students lean forward, smile and react naturally.
One student raises a hand:
**“Sir… can we actually race it?”**
Presenter:
**“Let’s find out.”**

### 42–55 sec — BUG
Student session is approved.
The car accelerates but fails to turn and crashes.
Natural student reactions.
Presenter calmly:
**“That’s not a failure. That’s our first bug.”**

### 55–75 sec — DEBUG
Center display exposes steering logic.
Left LCD shows input signal.
Right LCD shows vehicle response.
Presenter identifies duplicated steering input.
He makes one correction.
AI-assisted explanation appears only as a support layer, not as the creator.

### 75–95 sec — THE RACE
Students receive limited session access.
Cars launch.
Fast cinematic cuts: hands on devices, faces, displays, track, teacher reactions.
One student narrowly overtakes another.
Classroom energy rises.

### 95–108 sec — STRANGE SIGNAL
Race finishes.
For less than one second, all three displays flicker.
A tiny line appears:
**LEGACY CORE — ACCESS LIMITED**
Presenter notices it.
He says nothing.

### 108–115 sec — CLIFFHANGER
Center display returns to normal.
Presenter looks at the mobile.
The mobile briefly shows:
**UNKNOWN SOURCE: 2027**
CUT TO BLACK.

**END — PART 1**

## Performance / Motion QA
- No frozen people.
- Natural blinking, breathing, posture shifts and eye focus.
- Students react to events, not to the camera.
- Presenter gestures must have physical intent and visible UI response.
- Camera movement remains cinematic and motivated.
- No instant teleporting between positions.

## Continuity Locks
- Same presenter wardrobe and appearance across future classroom episodes.
- Same student group and classroom geography.
- Same three fixed displays.
- Same master mobile.
- Same Racing Game UI identity.
- No unauthorized Root/Secret Core reveal.

## Safety / Story Gate
Episode 1 reveals only a mysterious Legacy Core signal. The 2027 origin is shown as a brief source identifier, not explained. Full Mustafa reveal remains reserved for the later creator arc.
