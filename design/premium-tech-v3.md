# Premium Tech Style v3 — New Visual Design

## Purpose
Replace the legacy text-card look with a cinematic presenter-first composition for private review.

## Locked visual direction
- 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 FPS, 30 seconds.
- Full-body presenter is the visual anchor; face, hands, posture and clothing must remain natural.
- Presenter stage: dark premium studio, practical rim/key lighting, large 72-inch LCD behind/alongside presenter.
- LCD content: Letust gadget/product UI or contextual technology visuals.
- HUD: restrained glass panels, thin technical lines, small data labels and motion accents; never cover the presenter face/body.
- Typography: short headline + one supporting line; no paragraph blocks.
- Camera language: slow push, slight lateral drift, clean scene transitions; no unstable zoompan.
- Color language: deep charcoal/black studio base with cool cyan/white interface accents.
- Scene rhythm: Hook → Demonstration → Evidence/verification → Brand close.
- CTA is subtle and only appears at the end.
- Public YouTube publishing remains OFF; human visual/audio approval remains required.

## Scene layout
1. Hook: presenter full body, LCD wakes behind them, one-line question.
2. Demonstration: presenter gestures toward LCD while gadget/interface changes.
3. Verification: presenter remains visible while evidence cards/HUD appear on LCD.
4. Close: presenter centered, brand lockup and concise CTA.

## Asset policy
Prefer locally generated/open assets and free-stock footage where licensing is verified. No copyrighted celebrity likenesses, logos, or unverified footage.

## QA
The production workflow must still enforce MP4/container, resolution, duration, black-frame/freeze, voice, and captions checks. A successful CI run is not a substitute for human visual/audio approval.
